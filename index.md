## Featured Projects

### 1. Global Energy Transition and Data Compliance Panel Analysis

- **Project Name**: Global Energy Transition and Environmental Data Compliance Panel Analysis (2015-2023)
- **Research Question**: How do national economic metrics and environmental data compliance indices correlate with renewable energy adoption and structural carbon emission reduction across multi-year panel datasets?

- **Visualizations & Enhanced Analytical Insights (Based on Peer Feedback)**:

  - **Visualization 1 (2023 Energy Transition Multivariate Bubble Chart)**:
    This multi-dimensional scatter/bubble chart maps renewable energy shares against carbon emissions for 10 European nations in 2023. Bubble sizes represent GDP per capita, while the color gradient reflects the national Data Compliance Index.
    * **Key Analytical Insights & Reflection**: The analysis demonstrates a positive alignment between stringent data compliance standards and accelerated renewable energy integration. Higher compliance scores correlate with reduced carbon intensity, proving that data compliance is a strategic catalyst for sustainable development rather than merely an administrative overhead.

    ![Bubble Chart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hytt1123/data-science-project/main/complex_energy_bubble_chart.png)

  - **Visualization 2 (2015-2023 Compliance Trend Line Chart)**:
    This longitudinal line chart tracks the multi-year trajectory of environmental data compliance indices across selected European nations from 2015 to 2023.
    * **Key Analytical Insights & Reflection**: The longitudinal trajectory reveals a steady upward convergence in regulatory data compliance across European economies. This long-term trend underscores that standardized data governance architectures serve as foundational infrastructure for predictable green policy execution.

    ![Line Chart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hytt1123/data-science-project/main/compliance_trend_line_chart.png)

- **Future Roadmap (Addressing Interactivity Limitations)**:
  In response to peer evaluation, future iterations will transition these static visualizations into interactive `Plotly` and `Streamlit` dashboards, enabling real-time geospatial filtering and dynamic panel regression exploration.
